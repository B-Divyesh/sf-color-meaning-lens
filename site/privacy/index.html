Privacy note · effective 27 August 2026

What stays private.

Color Meaning Lens is designed so your work pages and color mappings do not need a server.

Extension data

Your per-site hostnames, color values, labels, patterns, enabled state, and optional named presets are stored locally in your browser. They are not sent to us. The extension reads computed styles from the page to draw the lens; it does not capture screenshots, read cross-origin canvas pixels, or alter source data.

License data

If you buy or restore a supporter license, the token is stored locally and sent to the Sociobot billing API solely to verify whether it is valid. Sociobot / Dodo is the merchant of record and processes checkout information under its own policies. We do not add analytics or advertising trackers.

Website and offline cache

The site uses no analytics cookies. Its service worker may cache public pages and assets on your device for faster, offline access. Removing site data clears that cache.

Your control

Remove extension data through your browser's extension storage controls or by uninstalling the extension. Clear site data to remove a license token saved on this website. Questions can be filed in the public source repository.
