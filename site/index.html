03 / METHOD

A 30-second setup for each site.

  1. Pick

    Open the extension and sample a page color with the eyedropper or element picker.

  2. Name

    Choose a short note, symbol, pattern, and a forgiving shade tolerance.

  3. Read

    The lens adds removable marks. Press Alt + Shift + L any time.

What it does not do

It is not medical color correction, does not infer meaning, does not edit images, and never claims your labels are ground truth.

04 / INSTALL

Load the unpacked extension.

  1. Download and unzip the package.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose “Load unpacked,” and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the lens. Open a site, then pick your first color.
